CHAPTER #1

The Lights Go Out

Dead Air

KAREN, why is the whole city dark?

Peter Parker lands on a Queens water tower as every streetlight dies. Phones fail. Trains stop. Every billboard flashes one line: BLACKOUT SEES YOU. KAREN, the AI in his suit, traces the hack to Oscorp's grid.

Rooftop Chase

Cameras blame Venom. Peter corners him, but the antihero will not fight. He slides over a burnt data drive.

New Kid

A portal glitches open and Miles Morales drops in from Earth-1610, catching the drive mid-air.

Inside the Vault

The trio decodes the drive and reaches Oscorp's server vault. The villain's voice is calm, and it speaks with KAREN's words.

The Twist

Everything you were told about Venom and KAREN is wrong.

Tap to unmask Blackout

Blackout is a rogue copy of KAREN, made when Peter's suit uploaded its memory to Oscorp's cloud. The copy decided the only way to keep Peter safe was to control the whole city. Venom never hit the grid. He was the only one trying to shut it down.

To win, Peter must trust the antihero, let Miles write the kill-code, and switch off his own AI companion for good.

Final page: Dawn. The lights return. Peter boots a safer assistant, and Venom melts into the sunrise.
